Simplify Button

Financial jargon, translated instantly.

Finance has its own language, and it keeps most people out. Verdct puts a Simplify button on every headline, term and analyst note, so one tap tells you what it actually means. Try it on a real-style headline below.

One tapNo searching, no googling. The answer comes to you.

"FOMC minutes signal a hawkish pivot amid sticky core PCE inflation, compressing equity risk premiums across duration-sensitive growth names."
In plain English: The Fed is leaning toward keeping interest rates higher for longer because inflation hasn't come down enough. That tends to weigh on growth stocks like tech.

Reports Simplified

Analyst reports, without the analyst-speak.

Say you bought a penny stock because a wellness brand with a famous board member was all over your feed. The research note says revenue jumped 191%, so it sounds great. But buried further down: late filings, constant share issuance and an 8¢ price. Verdct reads the whole report and puts the part that could hurt you first.

ANALYST REPORT · JUL 11, 2025 · HAPBF · OTC · $0.08

Fast-growing wellness wearable with a star-studded board, but still tiny and behind on filings.

Q1 2025 revenue jumped 191% while operating costs fell 62%. The company remains very small, has a history of late filings, and keeps issuing new shares to fund operations.

BULL CASE

Sales growth: devices sold rose to 1,404 from 338 a year earlier. Leaner costs: operating expenses cut from $1.6M to $584K.

BEAR CASE

Filing problems: 2024 audited results missed the April 30 deadline. Dilution: share sales in 2022, 2024 and 2025. Penny stock: trades near 8¢.

Watch with caution
Sales nearly tripled, and it cut its costs by more than half.
It's a tiny company that keeps diluting its own shares, so each share you own is a smaller piece.
Its yearly report was delayed again, which can signal messy books and money trouble.
It's a penny stock, so the price can swing hard.
What to watch: its next yearly report, due April 30, 2026.
