Apple faces a near-term demand headwind on iPhone 18 per Bank of America, while Qualcomm and Meta's AI agent initiatives threaten to erode its hardware premium. AMZNCAUTION· reuters.comSep 23, 2026
10-year Treasury yield tops 5%, its highest since 2007

The benchmark 10-year yield jumped about 9 basis points to 5.05% on Wednesday after S&P Global's flash U.S. Composite PMI rose to 58.4 in September from 56.0, its strongest reading since July 2021.

Higher yields raise the discount rate applied to future earnings, a headwind for richly valued growth stocks. The Nasdaq fell 1.05%, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.53%.

Read more →
What this means for you
  • The 10-year Treasury yield is the interest rate on U.S. government bonds. It just topped 5%, its highest since 2007.
  • When safe bonds pay more, investors are less willing to pay high prices for stocks.
  • Fast-growing stocks like Amazon tend to feel that most.
  • The tech-heavy Nasdaq fell about 1%, twice as much as the S&P 500.
  • This story was about interest rates, not Amazon's business.
