Financial jargon, translated instantly.

The Simplify button is Verdct's signature feature. Tap it on any headline, term, or analyst note and get a plain-English translation in seconds. Try it below.

One tapNo searching, no googling. The answer comes to you.
For any levelWhether you're brand new or been investing for years.
From today's news
"FOMC minutes signal a hawkish pivot amid sticky core PCE inflation, compressing equity risk premiums across duration-sensitive growth names."
In plain English: The Fed is leaning toward keeping interest rates higher for longer because inflation hasn't come down enough. This is bad news for growth stocks (like tech), which tend to lose value when rates stay elevated.
